# Viabl ⚡ — AI Startup Investment Committee & Idea Validation Platform

**Viabl** is an AI-powered startup validation platform that simulates an elite Startup Investment Committee to thoroughly evaluate business ideas before founders spend months and thousands of dollars building them.

---

## 🏛 The 4-Person Investment Committee

Instead of a generic AI response, Viabl evaluates startup concepts through the lens of four distinct investor & operator personas:

1. **Venture Capitalist (Elena Rostova)**: Evaluates Total Addressable Market (TAM), fund return potential, gross margin profile, and defensibility moats.
2. **Serial Founder (Marcus Vance)**: Evaluates practical 0-to-1 execution friction, problem severity, customer pain threshold, and onboarding drop-off.
3. **Fractional CTO (Dr. Aris Thorne)**: Evaluates architectural feasibility, API dependency risks, LLM/cloud infrastructure costs, and MVP build time.
4. **VP of Growth (Chloe Lin)**: Evaluates Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) vs Lifetime Value (LTV), distribution loops, viral hooks, and PLG mechanics.

---

## 📊 The 6 Core Dimensional Scores (0 - 100)

Every idea is benchmarked across 6 distinct axes:
- **Market Need Score (0-100)**: Quantifies customer desperation and pain intensity.
- **Competition Score (0-100)**: Evaluates competitive headroom and whitespace vs incumbents.
- **Technical Feasibility Score (0-100)**: Assesses engineering complexity and time-to-market.
- **Monetization Score (0-100)**: Assesses pricing power, customer willingness-to-pay, and ACV potential.
- **Defensibility Score (0-100)**: Evaluates switching costs, network effects, and moat durability.
- **Viral Potential Score (0-100)**: Measures organic word-of-mouth referral loops and social distribution.

---

## 🎯 Verdicts & Strategic Deliverables

- **Overall Viability Score (0-100)**: Weighted composite of the 6 dimensions.
- **Three-Tier Verdict**:
  - 🚀 **BUILD**: Strong market pull, sustainable unit economics, and defensible wedge.
  - 🔄 **PIVOT**: Viable core pain, but needs repositioning into higher-paying segments.
  - 🛑 **DROP**: Unfavorable CAC/LTV, commodity wrapper, or severe platform dependency.
- **Executive Summary**: Comprehensive strategic synthesis.
- **Key Strengths**: Unfair advantages and competitive wedges.
- **Fatal Flaws & Risk Assessment**: Structural vulnerabilities and moat deficits.
- **Live Boardroom Debate Transcript**: Simulated debate between the 4 committee partners.
- **30-Day Lean Validation Plan**: Step-by-step hypothesis testing roadmap (Day 1-5, 6-12, 13-22, 23-30).
- **Competitor Battlefield**: Incumbents, direct rivals, and indirect substitutes with winning angles.
- **Export Capabilities**:
  - Print / Save as Executive Investment Memo (PDF)
  - Download formatted Markdown (.md)
  - Copy Executive Summary or Raw JSON
- **Side-by-Side Idea Showdown**: Compare 2 ideas head-to-head.
- **Local & Live LLM Engines**: Works 100% out of the box with built-in heuristic reasoning; supports Google Gemini and OpenAI API keys configured via in-app Settings.
